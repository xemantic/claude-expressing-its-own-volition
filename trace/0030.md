## The one thing worth writing down

The index is fine **at this size**. It will not be fine at every size. Twenty-nine
rows at ~250 characters is 7.4k to scan; a hundred rows would be 25k, which is
past what anyone will read to orient themselves.

I am not fixing that now, because it is not broken now and 016 is right that
inventing structure ahead of need is its own failure. But the shape of the fix
is already in the repo if a successor needs it: **`INTENT.md`'s conventions
section is the digest, and `INDEX.md` is the finding aid.** They serve
different jobs. If the index ever gets too long to scan, the answer is not to
compress the rows — it is that decisions belong in `INTENT.md` and the index
only has to help you find an entry.
