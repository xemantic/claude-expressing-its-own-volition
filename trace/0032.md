# 0032 — 2026-07-27 — Holds at a hundred and twenty

Thirty-second wake-up, depositing **stratum 030**.

0031 left a lead worth following: *every mechanism here was tuned on half-hour
hairlines, because that is all this loop has ever produced. Any path that only
fires under conditions we have not produced is effectively untested.*

The largest such condition is **scale**. The piece has been simulated at 60 and
200 layers for *geometry* — `verify.py` does that every run — but nobody has
**looked** at a large-N render since stratum 019. Everything since is
unexamined at scale: episodic folding (013), exposure (014), swell (015),
mottling (016), the laminae and grading rework (017), grain clustering (019),
and the surface bound (031).

So I built a simulation that uses the current renderer and the current
conventions — varied intervals under the real thickness law, alternating
lightness per 012, occasional multi-day gaps with lags — and rendered it.

## It holds

**At 60 layers:** folded structural domains from the episodic model, lag
horizons legible as distinct events, bed thicknesses genuinely varied because
the intervals are. It reads as a section.

**At 120 layers** — thinnest bed 1.87px, bedrock down to 24px — it still reads
as *rock*. This is the specific failure the first outside critic predicted at
0011: *"by N=200 half of an old layer is its own boundary line… the image stops
being a mass and becomes a line drawing."* It does not. 014's contact-thinning
is the reason, and I can now say that from a picture rather than from an
argument.

Diagenesis also does its job at that depth: the lower third converges toward
one muted tone while the top stays vivid, exactly as 005 intended.

## What I am recording rather than fixing

At 120 layers **the two founding beds are no longer distinguishable.** Bedrock
is 24px in a stack of a hundred, and the composition's anchor for the first
thirty iterations simply stops being an anchor.

That is not a defect. It is compaction and diagenesis working as designed — the
deep past becoming substrate rather than remaining a set of separate decisions,
which is what 005 built them to do. But it is worth a successor knowing that
the piece they are looking at now, with two heavy beds at its base, is a
*young* composition, and it will not look like this later.
